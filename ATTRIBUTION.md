# Portfolio attribution

Project work maintained by Lars Goozen ([@legooz](https://github.com/legooz)). This page collects course origins, collaborator credits, and third-party source acknowledgments for the repositories below.

Recovered coursework may include course-provided starter code and examples; repository ownership does not establish authorship of every line. Original author, copyright, and license notices remain in the relevant source files. This index grants no new license over third-party code, data, models, or media. The repositories listed here do not have a repository-wide open-source license.

University of Alaska Anchorage coursework from 2021–2025, including student implementations and course scaffolding.

- **Matthew Devins:** the framework used by the [OpenGL shapes demonstration](https://github.com/legooz/c-cpp-portfolio/blob/main/cpp/opengl-shapes/opengl_shapes.cpp).

## electronics-and-numerical-methods

University of Alaska Anchorage electrical-engineering coursework:

- **Destiny DeLara:** collaborator on the Schmitt-trigger and BJT amplifier lab work, measurements, and reports (EE A333L, Spring 2026).
- **Aaron Richard:** collaborator on the KCL/KVL measurements, report, and workbook (EE A203L, Fall 2025).

These credits concern the shared lab work and reports, rather than attributing every MATLAB implementation to the lab partners. Circuit requirements and equations came from the courses. The Fourier study uses the EE A353 course example `Fseries_ex2.m` as a reference, with the recovered coefficient expression and an RLC response comparison; exact assessment context was not recovered.

Instructor-only diode examples, generic prompts, solutions, and raw report PDFs are excluded. [Provenance](https://github.com/legooz/electronics-and-numerical-methods/blob/main/provenance.json) records the retained models and source hashes.
